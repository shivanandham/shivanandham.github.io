## Context

Undivided supports parents of children with disabilities in the United States. Parents ask long, specific questions, and the answers had to come from Undivided's own curated knowledge base rather than the open web. This was my first production RAG system, built as a Francium Tech engineer.

## What I built

- Retrieval and re-ranking over pgvector, serving answers from a FastAPI backend with OpenAI and Azure OpenAI models.
- Feedback loops on answers.
- Accessible, SEO-focused Next.js pages for the public side of the product.

## Outcome

Shipped as part of the Undivided product. The same retrieval-and-re-ranking shape, with stronger grounding controls, went on to become the [Teldat assistant](/work/teldat-rag-assistant/).
