## Context

B-Cube.ai runs trading bots for its users: a signal fires, and orders go out on each subscribed user's exchange account. The existing platform was a Node.js/Angular codebase that had to be rebuilt, with existing users, paid subscriptions and live trading carried across. I led the rebuild as a Francium Tech engineer.

## Constraints

- **Nothing could be lost in transit**: users authenticated through legacy FusionAuth, active subscriptions, and bots trading real money.
- **Eight exchanges**, spot and futures, each with its own API and, for Binance, a real-time WebSocket feed.
- **Regulated edges**: KYC before trading, two-factor authentication, and payment both by card subscription and by the platform's own ERC-20 token.
- **Analytics on time series** (returns, drawdown, profit factor) across every bot and portfolio.

## What I led and built

- **The Rails 8 API and Vue 3 frontend** that replaced the Node.js/Angular stack.
- **Migration of legacy FusionAuth users**, with 2FA and KYC-gated authorisation in the new system.
- **Signal fan-out into per-user executions** placing orders through AWS Lambda across 8 exchanges (Binance with real-time WebSocket, KuCoin, Kraken, Bybit and more), spot and futures.
- **Bot and portfolio analytics** as parameterised SQL over AWS Timestream: returns, drawdown, profit factor.
- **Sumsub KYC and Chargebee subscriptions** with idempotent webhooks, and BCUBE ERC-20 token payments verified on-chain through Etherscan.

## The hard part

Fan-out. One signal becomes one execution per subscribed user, each placing orders on that user's own exchange account through AWS Lambda, across eight exchanges with different APIs and both spot and futures markets. Keeping those executions per user is also what makes the analytics honest: bot and portfolio returns, drawdown and profit factor are computed as parameterised SQL over Timestream. The webhooks from Sumsub and Chargebee follow the same discipline: they are idempotent, so a replayed event cannot be applied twice.

## Outcome

- The platform re-launched on Rails 8 and Vue 3 with its users, subscriptions and trading carried over.
- Order execution on 8 exchanges, spot and futures; analytics over Timestream; KYC, subscriptions and token payments integrated.

## Stack
